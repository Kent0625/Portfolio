Analytics Framework

EDA & Data Storytelling Studio

Turning raw and unstructured data collections into clean, validated, explainable visual artifacts.

The 4-Stage Analytical Workflow

Every dataset passes through an audit pipeline before statistical modeling or machine learning begins.

Stage 01
Ingest & Clean
Missing value imputation, type casting, text normalization, and deduplication.
Stage 02
Profile & Explore
Skewness evaluation, correlation matrices, outlier thresholds, and distribution analysis.
Stage 03
Model & Validate
Cross-validation, XGBoost/Tree baselines, hyperparameter sweeps, and holdout tests.
Stage 04
Story & Deploy
Interactive Streamlit spaces, decision KPI cards, and stakeholder research posters.
